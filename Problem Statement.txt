Laptop Price Prediction

1. Problem Statement

Laptop prices depend on various specifications such as brand, processor, RAM, storage, GPU, screen size, and operating system. It is difficult to determine the appropriate price manually. Therefore, a machine learning system is developed to predict the price of a laptop based on its specifications.

2. Identify AI/ML Type
AI/ML: Machine Learning
Learning Type: Supervised Learning
Problem Type: Regression
Algorithms: Linear Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest

3. Objective

To develop a supervised machine learning model that predicts the price of a laptop based on its technical specifications such as brand, processor, RAM, storage, GPU, and display.

Input: Laptop Specifications
Output: Predicted Laptop Price
